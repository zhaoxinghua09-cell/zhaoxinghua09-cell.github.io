Lifecycle Governance Doctrine (LGD)

Evidence-Gated AI Lifecycle Governance: governance of any autonomous system — AI, robots, autonomous vehicles, low-altitude systems, data assets — as a full-lifecycle chain from birth to retirement, not single-point checks.

Registered — registry at birth Evidenced — evidence for every key act Gated — gates before every evolution
